# Needs rules check

One `RULING:` line per ambiguity: the rule, what we did, and why. The owner reconciles them in one sitting.

Where two lines cover the same rule, the later line supersedes the earlier one.

RULING: Front-loaded initiative (INIT-005) | At the start of each loser/winner pair, a side with more remaining units selects ceil(own/other); otherwise 1 | Changelog P5 gives only the name; LITKO and a battletech.com forum thread describe front-loading as the larger side's extra moves coming first (3 vs 4: L WW L W L W); AGoAC's back-loaded table uses floor
RULING: Punch table reversal (HITLOC-006) | Rows 1-5 reversed in every column (front 1 RA,2 RT,3 CT,4 LT,5 LA; left 1-2 LA,3 CT,4-5 LT; right 1-2 RA,3 CT,4-5 RT), head kept on 6 | CL C18 only says it now matches the standard table; this puts low rolls on the right for front attacks and on the near limbs for side attacks
RULING: Charge damage (PHYS-043) | ceil(tonnage/5 x (TMM+1)), TMM taken from hexes moved +1 for the target hex | CL C24 says the formula is TMM-based and counts the target hex but gives no formula; this matches the brief and roughly the old tonnage/10 x hexes up to about 10 hexes, then lower
RULING: Displacement timing (PHYS-090) | A Displacement Step at the end of the Physical Attack Phase, before that phase's PSR queue | CL C19 says only that displacement cannot cancel other physical attacks; the brief's 'end of turn' would put the Heat Phase between a push and its PSR
RULING: Ammo explosion with CASE / CASE II (AMMO-011/012) | CASE: cap 10, confined to the location, rear armor lost. CASE II: 1 structure damage, the rest to armor, -1 to crit checks there | CL D11 gives the 20 cap and says CASE results are new without numbers; the CASE 10 cap comes from the brief
